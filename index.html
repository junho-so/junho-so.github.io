Junho So

Hi! I am a fourth-year undergraduate student in the Department of Mathematics at Ajou University in Suwon, South Korea.

Research

I study the dynamics of optimization in deep learning, aiming to connect theoretical analysis with practical training. In particular, I focus on understanding how the noise in mini-batch gradients and the update rule influence training behavior (e.g., implicit bias, sharpness, and the edge of stability).
